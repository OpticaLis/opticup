## 2026-05-06 — M6 prescription_glasses vs prescription_contacts — TWO entities, not one with discriminator


**Situation:** After agreeing to split exam from prescription, next question: is "prescription" one entity with `type='glasses'/'contacts'`, or two separate entities?
**My recommendation:** Two separate entities. Reason: the field sets are largely disjoint — glasses have prism/base/PD-distance/add-for-reading, contacts have BC/DIA/lens-type/brand-spec. A single entity would be 70%+ NULL fields. Each entity also has its own internal discriminator (glasses: distance/reading/multi-focal/bi-focal/office; contacts: daily/monthly/hard).
**Daniel's response:** Asked me first whether I had taken into account that they look different — I had, but hadn't explained well. After clarification, agreed.
**Reason for agreement:** The two-entity design respects domain reality — they ARE different prescriptions, not variants of the same prescription.
**Lesson:** When proposing structural split, lead with WHY — show the distinct field sets, not just "they're different". Daniel will trust the recommendation if he sees the reasoning, not just the conclusion. Pattern: "for entity-split decisions, always include the disjoint-fields evidence in the first message".
