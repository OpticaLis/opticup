## Top findings (Brief asks for "Top 3")

### 1. M4-V2-SESSION-RESTORE-01 — Session-saved selections do NOT persist across modal reopens (showAsync path)

- **What:** Operator deselects 2 recipients, clicks cancel, reopens the same rule's modal — the deselections are NOT restored. The save side writes correctly to `sessionStorage`; the load side never reads them back when invoked from `showAsync` (the only path operator UI uses).
- **Root cause:** `_ensureState(null, onChoice)` is called BEFORE the preview promise resolves; `_loadSession(null)` fails the rule-key match and returns null; `_hydrate(modal, pv)` does not re-attempt `_loadSession` after the preview arrives.
- **Severity:** Medium — UX regression of Brief §3.3 cross-cutting test #1. Not a safety/data issue.
- **Suggested fix:** In `_hydrate`, after setting `_state.recipients`, call `_loadSession(pv)` (now has rules array) and merge the returned `excluded` Set into `_state.excluded`, then run the existing stale-id reconciliation.
- **Where:** `modules/crm/crm-confirm-send-v2.js:249-263` (the `_hydrate` function)

### 2. Cascade behavior: soft-deleting a lead also soft-deletes its attendee rows and may flip 'cancelled' status

- **What:** When the Pipeline soft-deleted `דניאל טסט` lead at 02:59:35Z to free the phone for the lead_intake test, FIVE of its attendee rows simultaneously got `is_deleted=true` and (3 of them) `status='cancelled'`. After restoring the lead's `is_deleted=false`, the attendees were NOT auto-restored — needed a separate UPDATE.
- **Severity:** Low — operational risk if anyone soft-deletes a lead with active attendees expecting the operation to be reversible. The cascade is presumably intentional (an attendee record without a parent lead doesn't make sense in the live UI) but is one-directional.
- **Suggested follow-up:** Document the cascade in `MODULE_SPEC.md` for Module 4, and consider whether un-delete should also un-delete the attendees.
- **Where:** likely a DB trigger or RPC on `crm_leads.is_deleted` update; not part of M4_DRY_RUN_PREVIEW work but surfaced during validation.

### 3. Pre-flight whitelist superset (same as prior Pipeline overnight) — `danylis92@gmail.com` is in demo DB email allowlist but not on Brief §4.2 whitelist

- **What:** demo tenant's `ui_config.test_mode_email_allowlist` contains 3 emails; the Brief lists 2 (lacks `danylis92@gmail.com`). Pipeline applied recipient discipline (never selected `danylis92@gmail.com` as test recipient) and continued.
- **Severity:** LOW. Same recommendation as the prior escalation: Daniel picks between (a) keep DB allowlist as-is + update future Briefs, or (b) tighten DB allowlist to match the Brief.
